Downloads

CourtConnect is available as an Android app, an iPhone & iPad app and an offline ISO — so the whole legal toolkit works even without the internet.

🤖

Android App (APK)

Android app with the full CourtConnect site bundled inside — opens instantly and works completely offline. Official court links open in your browser. Version 1.2.2.

Open this page in Chrome first. Apps like WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook can't install APKs from their built-in browser — tap ⋮ → Open in Chrome.
  • Filecourtconnect-v1.2.2.apk · ~4.1 MB
  • RequiresAndroid 5.0 or newer — including Android 14, 15, 16 & 17
  • Step 1Tap Download APK. If Chrome warns about the file, tap Download anyway
  • Step 2Open the downloaded file. When asked, allow “Install unknown apps” for Chrome, then go back
  • Step 3Tap Install. If Play Protect warns, tap More details → Install anyway
Seeing “App not installed”? You're opening an old CourtConnect file or have an old version installed. Uninstall any CourtConnect app, delete old courtconnect…apk files from your Downloads folder, then download again — the working file is courtconnect-v1.2.2.apk.
⬇ Download APK
📱 This file is for Android phones only. On iPhone or iPad, follow the iPhone & iPad steps above — no download needed.

iPhone & iPad (iOS)

Install CourtConnect from Safari — it gets its own home-screen icon, opens full-screen like a native app and keeps the core pages for offline use. No App Store needed.

Open this page in Safari first. Apps like WhatsApp, Instagram and Gmail can't add it to your Home Screen — tap ⋯ or the compass icon → Open in Safari.
  • Step 1Open this page in Safari (not inside WhatsApp, Instagram or Gmail)
  • Step 2Tap Share (square with ↑ arrow) — at the bottom on iPhone, or inside the ⋯ menu on newer iPhones; top-right on iPad
  • Step 3Scroll down, choose “Add to Home Screen”, then tap Add
  • RequiresiPhone or iPad with Safari (iOS / iPadOS 13+)

💿

Offline ISO (bootable)

Bootable Linux ISO that starts a local server and opens CourtConnect in the browser — the full site, no internet needed. Great for court complexes, libraries and kiosks.

  • Filecourtconnect-offline.iso
  • Size~15 MB
  • UseBurn to DVD / write to USB (Ventoy, Rufus) / attach to a VM
  • BootBoots to console; open http://localhost:8080 in the browser

No downloads needed? CourtConnect is also an installable web app — on Android Chrome, open the site, tap the menu ⋮ → “Add to Home screen”; on iPhone / iPad, use Safari's Share → “Add to Home Screen”. You'll get an app icon without any APK.
