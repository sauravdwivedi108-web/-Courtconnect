The books

Bare Acts Library

The books of all the acts, grouped by subject. Every title links to the official text on India Code (indiacode.nic.in), the Legislative Department or the Gazette of India.

A note on the new criminal laws: the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) — all 2023 — replaced the IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act with effect from 1 July 2024. Pre-2024 citations still matter for older cases, and the repealed statutes remain relevant in practice.
