District Court Services

Six daily-use services across all 11 districts of Delhi. Case-status and cause-list searches run on the national eCourts portal; district-specific lists (judges, on-leave, rosters) open the official website of the district you select.

Quick tip: the same district website also hosts case status, cause lists, court orders and video-conference links — choose any district below whenever a card asks you to.
⚖️

Case Status

Track any case in Delhi's district courts by CNR, case number, FIR, party or advocate name — on the national eCourts portal.

🧑‍⚖️

Judges List

Current judicial officers in every district — Principal District & Sessions Judge, District Judges, ASJs and Chief/Additional Chief Judicial Magistrates with room numbers.

📋

Causelist

Day-wise cause lists of the Delhi district courts — via the national eCourts gateway or your district court's own page.

Open eCourts
🌴

Judges on Leave

Which judicial officers are on leave, from when to when, and the nature of leave — updated by each district court.

🔒

Bail Roster

Bail duty rosters and bail-matter cause lists notified by district courts — usually issued with the monthly duty rosters.

🕘

Duty Magistrate Roster

Monthly duty rosters of Judicial Magistrates First Class — regular, evening, weekend-traffic and jail duty rosters.
